Tool 1

Strategy Dashboard

Copy the prompt, fill in the brackets, and paste it into Muse.

Why it matters: You get one clear plan for what to post and when, so you can show up for your people with confidence.

Prompt · Strategy Dashboard 
Please build me a social media strategy for [Facebook or Instagram].

Step 1 — The account I want to learn from:
- Name: [___]
- Handle or profile link: [___]

Step 2 — About me:
- What I'm promoting: [___] (my book, product, service, or ministry)
- Who I serve: [___]
- My page or profile link: [___]
- My goal: [___] (more customers, more bookings, more engagement, more sign-ups)
- Time I have each week: [___]
- My voice: [___] (warm, professional, funny, plain-spoken)

Please do this:
1. Look at both accounts and compare them: what each talks about, which formats get attention, where I stand apart, and what my audience needs.
2. Turn what you find into my strategy: my content focus (3 to 4 themes), a 10-day playbook, quick wins I can do this week, and my next steps.
3. Give me 10 to 12 post ideas I can use right away, in my voice.
4. Give me a simple keyword funnel: one word people can comment to get my link by direct message.
5. Tell me what NOT to do — the mistakes that would cost me reach.
6. Build all of this into one shareable dashboard page I can open and share, with the comparison, my content focus, 10-day playbook, post ideas, keyword funnel, what NOT to do, quick wins, and next steps.

Tool 2

Customer Language Bank

Copy the prompt, fill in the brackets, and paste it into Muse.

Why it matters: People buy when they feel understood. The fastest way to show you understand them is to use their exact words.

Prompt · Customer Language Bank 
I sell [product] to [target customer].

The main problem it solves is [problem].

Search these Facebook Groups and Instagram accounts: [list 2–3 groups or accounts].

Collect at least 30 real quotes, exactly as written.

Group them into frustrations, desired outcomes, objections, and things already tried that did not work.

For each theme, list the 3 phrases that come up most often.

Then give me 10 ad hooks in their wording, not marketing language.

Weekly update: every Monday, check the same groups and accounts for the past week and add new phrases.

Then create a dashboard: build all of this into one shareable dashboard page I can open and share.

Optional: Keep the weekly update line only if you want Muse to check back every Monday. Delete it if you only want a one-time search.

Tool 3

7 Day Content Calendar

Copy the prompt, fill in the brackets, and paste it into Muse.

Why it matters: Consistency builds trust, and a plan beats willpower. When the week is already drafted, showing up is easy.

Prompt · 7 Day Content Calendar 
Create a 7 day content calendar for [event, service, book, etc. name].

Use these 4 themes: education, stories, proof, and invitations.

Give me one post idea per day with a hook, a main point, and a call to action.

Also suggest an image and the prompt to create the image for each day.

Then create a dashboard: build all of this into one shareable dashboard page I can open and share.

Action Step: Run the prompt

Tool 4

Finding Your Next Clients One Conversation at a Time

Choose one: search Facebook or search Instagram. Copy the prompt, fill in the brackets, and paste it into Muse.

Why it matters: Your next clients are already asking for help in groups and feeds you’re part of. A helpful answer beats a cold pitch.

Option A Search Facebook

Prompt · Search Facebook 
Using what you know about my business or project [or add information here about your business or project]

Search the Facebook group [name of my group] that I am a member of, or groups I should join if I am not already a member, for recent posts where someone is asking for help, a recommendation, or advice about these topics: [the things my people ask about].

Show me up to 10 matching posts. For each one, give me: a one-line summary of what the person asked, why it could be a good fit for what I offer, and the direct link to the post.

Skip posts where the person is selling something themselves, and skip anything that looks like an argument. Most promising first, please.

For any group I am not a member of yet, tell me so I can join it.

Then create a dashboard: build all of this into one shareable dashboard page I can open and share.
